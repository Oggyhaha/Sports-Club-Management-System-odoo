# Owner Dashboard Metrics & Executive KPIs (Milestone G)

## 1. Executive Dashboard Overview

The **Unified Owner Dashboard** (`GET /api/v1/reports/dashboard`) provides a single comprehensive view of club health across all business verticals. It is restricted exclusively to authenticated users with the `owner` role.

---

## 2. Metric Catalog

### 2.1 Financial Health & Cash Flow
- **Invoices Issued Value**: Total monetary value of invoices raised in the period.
- **Outstanding Receivables**: Real-time balance due across open invoices.
- **Net Customer Collections**: Cash received less customer refunds in the period.
- **Recorded Operating Expenses**: Sum of payroll disbursements made in the period.
- **Estimated Operating Surplus**: Net collections less recorded payroll disbursements.

### 2.2 Membership & Retention
- **Total Registered Members**: All-time registered members.
- **Active Members**: Members with active status and valid plan expiry.
- **Active Subscriptions**: Currently active subscription contracts.
- **Renewals in Period**: Members purchasing subsequent subscriptions.
- **Plan Distribution**: Breakdown of active members across Gold, Silver, and Junior tiers.

### 2.3 Court Operations & Utilization
- **Total Bookings**: Count of court bookings scheduled in the period.
- **Confirmed vs Cancelled Bookings**: Operational execution metrics.
- **Cancellation Rate (%)**: $\frac{\text{Cancelled}}{\text{Total}} \times 100$.
- **Walk-In Ratio (%)**: $\frac{\text{Walk-In Bookings}}{\text{Total Bookings}} \times 100$.
- **Court Utilization Rate (%)**:
  $$\text{Utilization} = \frac{\text{Confirmed Booked Hours}}{\text{Active Courts} \times \text{Days in Period} \times 16} \times 100$$
  *(Club operating hours: 06:00 to 22:00 = 16 hours/day/court)*.
- **Peak Hours**: Top 5 most frequented hour slots (e.g., 18:00, 19:00).

### 2.4 Commerce & Inventory Health
- **Orders Count**: Breakdown between Shop counter and Cafeteria kitchen.
- **Completed Orders Value**: Total turnover generated by retail sales.
- **Top Selling Products**: Highest velocity items by units sold and revenue.
- **Low Stock Alerts**: Count and list of products where `current_stock <= reorder_threshold`.

### 2.5 Human Resources & Payroll
- **Total & Active Headcount**: Distribution of staff across departments.
- **Attendance Records**: Count of present vs absent/half-day entries.
- **Leave Approvals**: Total applied vs approved leaves.
- **Payroll Disbursed**: Actual salary disbursements issued.
- **Outstanding Payroll Liability**: Net approved payroll payable minus disbursements.

### 2.6 Output Tax Liability
- **Taxable Turnover**: Retail orders turnover plus direct invoices turnover.
- **Output Tax Collected**: Output GST liability from orders and direct invoices (deduplicated).
